Black Truffle Agnolotti in Burnt Shallot Consommé
Source: adapted from Osteria Francescana and The French Laundry
Inspiring Chef: Massimo Bottura & Thomas Keller

Delicate ricotta-filled agnolotti served in a crystal-clear burnt shallot consommé with Parmigiano foam, frozen brown butter pearls, and shaved black truffle.

Serves 4

Ingredients:

  • Fresh Agnolotti (see sub-recipe)
  • Burnt Shallot Consommé (see sub-recipe)
  • Parmigiano Foam (see sub-recipe)
  • Brown Butter Pearls (see sub-recipe)
  • 1 whole – Fresh Black Truffle
  • small garnish – Micro Chervil
  • small garnish – Chive Batons
  • few drops – Cold-Pressed Olive Oil
  • to finish – Flaky Sea Salt

Steps:

  1. Prepare the Fresh Agnolotti dough and filling.
  2. Prepare the Burnt Shallot Consommé and clarify until crystal clear.
  3. Prepare the Brown Butter Pearls using the anti-griddle.
  4. Prepare the Parmigiano Foam and keep warm.
  5. Cook agnolotti in heavily salted boiling water for 90 seconds.
  6. Arrange agnolotti in a shallow bowl with brown butter pearls and foam.
  7. Finish with shaved black truffle, micro herbs, olive oil, and flaky salt.
  8. Pour hot consommé tableside beneath a smoked glass cloche before serving.

Fresh Agnolotti

Yield: 20-24 agnolotti

Ingredients:

  • 300 g – Italian 00 Flour
  • 2 – Whole Eggs
  • 4 – Egg Yolks
  • 10 ml – Olive Oil
  • 8 g – Sea Salt
  • 250 g – Sheep’s Milk Ricotta
  • 90 g – Parmigiano Reggiano
  • from 1 lemon – Lemon Zest
  • small pinch – Nutmeg

Steps:

  1. Combine flour, eggs, yolks, olive oil, and salt into a smooth pasta dough.
  2. Vacuum seal and rest dough for 1 hour.
  3. Blend ricotta, Parmigiano, lemon zest, and nutmeg until silky smooth.
  4. Roll pasta sheets thin and pipe filling in small lines.
  5. Fold, seal, and cut agnolotti using a ripple pasta wheel.

Burnt Shallot Consommé

Yield: 1 liter

Ingredients:

  • 8 – Shallots
  • 2 – Yellow Onions
  • 1 – Leek
  • 1 liter – Brown Chicken Stock
  • 150 ml – Dry Sherry
  • 1 sheet – Kombu
  • 3 – Thyme Sprigs
  • 1 teaspoon – Black Peppercorns
  • 2 – Egg Whites

Steps:

  1. Deeply char shallots, onions, and leek in a dry copper pot.
  2. Deglaze with sherry and add stock, kombu, thyme, and peppercorns.
  3. Simmer gently for 45 minutes.
  4. Clarify using whisked egg whites and strain through a chinois étamine.
  5. Reduce slightly until intensely aromatic.

Brown Butter Pearls

Yield: Small garnish batch

Ingredients:

  • 150 g – European Butter
  • pinch – Smoked Sea Salt

Steps:

  1. Brown butter until nutty and aromatic.
  2. Pipe droplets onto an anti-griddle to create frozen pearls.
  3. Season lightly with smoked sea salt and freeze until service.

Parmigiano Foam

Yield: 500 ml

Ingredients:

  • 250 ml – Heavy Cream
  • 120 g – Parmigiano Reggiano
  • 2 g – Soy Lecithin

Steps:

  1. Warm cream gently and infuse with Parmigiano for 20 minutes.
  2. Blend with soy lecithin until airy and smooth.
  3. Hold warm until plating.
