Charred Asparagus Wrapped in Prosciutto with Brown Butter Sabayon
Source: inspired by Osteria Francescana and Noma
Inspiring Chef: Massimo Bottura & René Redzepi

A Michelin-style starter featuring fire-charred asparagus wrapped in crisp prosciutto, paired with airy brown butter sabayon, preserved lemon gel, hazelnut soil, and black garlic ash.

Serves 4

Ingredients:

  • 16 – Thick asparagus spears
  • 8 slices – Prosciutto di Parma (thinly sliced)
  • 2 tbsp – Extra virgin olive oil
  • to taste – Maldon sea salt
  • to taste – Tellicherry black pepper (freshly cracked)
  • Brown Butter Sabayon (see sub-recipe)
  • Preserved Lemon Gel (see sub-recipe)
  • Black Garlic Ash (see sub-recipe)
  • Hazelnut Soil (see sub-recipe)
  • for garnish – Micro basil
  • for garnish – Bronze fennel fronds
  • for garnish – Chive blossoms
  • for garnish – Freeze-dried asparagus dust

Steps:

  1. Trim the woody ends from the asparagus and blanch in salted boiling water for 35 seconds.
  2. Shock the asparagus in ice water and dry thoroughly.
  3. Wrap each asparagus spear with prosciutto, leaving the tips exposed.
  4. Brush lightly with olive oil and season with sea salt and black pepper.
  5. Prepare the preserved lemon gel, black garlic ash, hazelnut soil, and brown butter sabayon.
  6. Heat the Binchotan grill to high heat and char the asparagus until lightly blistered and the prosciutto is crisp.
  7. Swipe preserved lemon gel onto the plate and pipe warm sabayon alongside.
  8. Arrange asparagus in a stacked presentation over hazelnut soil.
  9. Finish with black garlic ash, asparagus dust, micro basil, fennel fronds, and chive blossoms.
  10. Optional: Cover with a smoking cloche filled with applewood smoke before serving.

Brown Butter Sabayon

Yield: Enough for 4 plates

Ingredients:

  • 3 – Egg yolks
  • 90g – Cultured butter
  • 1 tbsp – Champagne vinegar reduction
  • 1 tbsp – Dry vermouth
  • 1 tsp – Lemon juice
  • pinch – Fine sea salt

Steps:

  1. Brown the butter until nutty and aromatic.
  2. Whisk egg yolks, vinegar reduction, and vermouth over a bain-marie until thick and airy.
  3. Slowly emulsify in the browned butter.
  4. Season with lemon juice and salt, then transfer to an iSi siphon.

Preserved Lemon Gel

Yield: 1 squeeze bottle

Ingredients:

  • 2 – Preserved lemons
  • 120ml – Water
  • 1 tsp – Agar agar
  • 1 tsp – Honey

Steps:

  1. Blend preserved lemons, water, and honey until smooth.
  2. Bring to a simmer and whisk in agar agar.
  3. Cool until set, then blend until silky smooth.

Black Garlic Ash

Yield: Small garnish batch

Ingredients:

  • 4 – Black garlic cloves
  • 1 sheet – Dehydrated rye bread
  • 1 tsp – Activated charcoal powder (food-safe)

Steps:

  1. Dehydrate black garlic until tacky and concentrated.
  2. Blend with rye bread and activated charcoal into a fine ash.
  3. Pass through a fine sieve.

Hazelnut Soil

Yield: 1 cup

Ingredients:

  • 60g – Toasted hazelnuts
  • 20g – Panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp – Brown butter powder
  • pinch – Smoked salt

Steps:

  1. Pulse hazelnuts, breadcrumbs, brown butter powder, and smoked salt until crumbly.
  2. Toast lightly in a pan until aromatic.
