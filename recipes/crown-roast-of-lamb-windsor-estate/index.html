Herb Crusted Crown Roast of Lamb

Yield: Serves 4

Ingredients:

  • 2 – Premium lamb racks
  • 2 tbsp – Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp – Lamb fat
  • 1 cup – Fresh parsley
  • 1/2 cup – Fresh mint
  • 1/2 cup – Fresh rosemary
  • 1 cup – Panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp – Juniper berries
  • 2 tsp – Sea salt
  • 1 tsp – Black pepper

Steps:

  1. Season lamb with salt and pepper.
  2. Vacuum seal with lamb fat and cook sous vide at 131°F (55°C) for 90 minutes.
  3. Blend herbs, breadcrumbs, lamb fat, and ground juniper berries.
  4. Sear lamb and brush with Dijon mustard.
  5. Form into a crown roast and coat with herb crust.
  6. Roast at 425°F (220°C) for 8–10 minutes.
  7. Rest 10 minutes before carving.

Smoked Pea Purée

Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients:

  • 500g – English peas
  • 100ml – Heavy cream
  • 50g – Butter

Steps:

  1. Blanch peas and shock in ice water.
  2. Blend with cream and butter until smooth.
  3. Pass through a fine chinois.
  4. Cold smoke with applewood for 5 minutes.

Madeira Lamb Jus

Yield: 2 cups

Ingredients:

  • 2 lbs – Lamb bones
  • 2 – Shallots
  • 2 – Carrots
  • 1 – Celery stalk
  • 2 tbsp – Tomato paste
  • 750ml – Lamb stock
  • 250ml – Madeira wine
  • 2 sprigs – Fresh thyme

Steps:

  1. Roast bones and vegetables until deeply caramelized.
  2. Add stock, Madeira, thyme, and tomato paste.
  3. Simmer 4–5 hours.
  4. Strain and reduce until glossy.
  5. Finish with cold butter.

Confit Shallots

Yield: 12 shallots

Ingredients:

  • 12 – Banana shallots
  • 250ml – Duck fat

Steps:

  1. Cook shallots submerged in duck fat at 185°F (85°C) for 2 hours.

Glazed Heritage Carrots

Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients:

  • 12 – Heritage baby carrots
  • 25g – Butter
  • 1 tbsp – Honey

Steps:

  1. Blanch carrots until tender.
  2. Glaze with butter and honey until shiny.

Mint Gel

Yield: 1 squeeze bottle

Ingredients:

  • 200ml – Mint juice
  • 2g – Agar agar

Steps:

  1. Simmer mint juice and agar.
  2. Chill until set.
  3. Blend smooth and transfer to squeeze bottle.
